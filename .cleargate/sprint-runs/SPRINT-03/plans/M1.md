### C2 — `src/lint/*`, `src/commands/lint.ts`, one line in `src/ingest/lint-hook.ts`, two lines in `src/program.ts` (owner STORY-005-02)

**Layout rules (binding for the owner and every consumer of C2):**
1. **INSERTION POINTs.**
   - A consumer adds its line directly below its own `// INSERTION POINT STORY-…` comment.
   - The owner keeps every INSERTION POINT line, and every `// (keep this line: …)` line, exactly as written. It also keeps the two check-module import lines above them.
   - The owner adds its own imports **above the first import line** of `checks.ts` and adds nothing below `RULE_CHECKS`.
   - Simulated while writing this plan: the owner's body and imports plus 005-03, 005-04 and 005-08 each inserting at their anchors merged cleanly in every order. Result: `[broken-link, uncited-claim, orphan-page, index-drift, unlogged-change, stale-fact]` and `RULE_CHECKS = [...RULES_LINT_CHECKS]`.
2. **No value import of `./checks.js` from a check module.**
   - `links.ts`, `citations.ts`, and later `unlogged.ts` (005-03), `staleness.ts` (005-08) and `rules-lint.ts` (005-04) import `checks.ts` with `import type` only.
   - They take `pagePath` from `./paths.js`.
   - Reason (verified with Node 25 ESM): `checks.ts` reads the check `const`s at top level (`PAGE_CHECKS = [brokenLinkCheck, …]`). If a check module value-imports `checks.ts` and is itself loaded first, `checks.ts` evaluates before the check module and throws `ReferenceError: Cannot access 'brokenLinkCheck' before initialization`. That happens to a unit test that imports `links.js` first, or to `ingest-finish.ts` (005-03) importing `pageHash` from `unlogged.js` before `checks.js`; the latter would break every CLI command at start-up.
   - `checks.ts` re-exports `pagePath`, so its frozen home is unchanged.
3. **`src/lint/paths.ts` is final.** No story edits it; 005-02's §3.2 `pagePath` formula is already its body.
